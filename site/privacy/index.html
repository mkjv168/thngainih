Privacy

Cookieless. We store no IP addresses or personal data. No account is needed. When enabled, GoatCounter counts page views and named events (quiz_started, quiz_finished, share_tapped) without cookies. It computes a short-lived hash of IP + User-Agent to count unique visitors and does not store the IP address. Analytics is disabled until deployment. The ?s= source tag records only which channel a link came from. Lunar dates and holidays are computed on your device. Your language preference is saved locally on your device.
